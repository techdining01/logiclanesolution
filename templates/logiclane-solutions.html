Selected work

A few systems we've built and still maintain.

Logistics & distribution Delta Freightways

A dispatch system that replaced four spreadsheets

Route planning, driver assignment and delivery proof-of-receipt in one internal tool, built to work offline on the yard floor where signal is unreliable.

Dispatch time down from 40 minutes to under 8 per shift.

Retail & payments Coral Market Group

Inventory and point-of-sale, unified across nine branches

Stock levels, transfers and daily reconciliation visible from one dashboard, replacing branch-by-branch manual counts.

Stock discrepancies down 70% in the first quarter.

Patient scheduling built around three clinic locations

Appointment booking, reminders and staff rostering, integrated with the clinics' existing billing software rather than replacing it.

No-show rate reduced from 22% to 9%.
